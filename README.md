# Scenario Vocab Coach 🇰🇷

Learn the Korean you actually need in Busan: whole phrases inside real situations, like ordering
at a café, taking the metro or a part-time job interview. You practice them until you can say
them without help. Made for international students at BUFS, in **English or 日本語**.

**▶️ Try it: [vocab-coach.streamlit.app](https://vocab-coach.streamlit.app/)**

No sign-up. Your progress is saved in your own browser, on this device.

## Getting started

1. Open [the app](https://vocab-coach.streamlit.app/) and pick a lesson on **🏠 Home**. Start with Level 1 if you're new to Korean.
2. Choose how you answer in the sidebar: **🎙️ Speak** (allow the microphone) or **⌨️ Type**.
3. Switch the language at the top of the sidebar (🌐 English / 日本語).

## A lesson in five tabs

| Tab | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **1 · Situation** | Listen to a real conversation. Tap any line to replay it, or slow it down. |
| **2 · Notice** | Phrase cards: audio, meaning, how polite it is, and a common mistake. |
| **3 · Recall** | See the meaning, then type or say the Korean. Hints help, but the phrase comes back sooner. |
| **4 · Role-play** | A new conversation in the same place. Reply to complete a task. |
| 📝 **Test** | A short test before and after studying, so you can see how much you learned. |

**Role-play has two modes:**

- **🗣️ Practice:** take your time.
- **⏱️ Time attack:** they speak, the recorder starts by itself, and you have a few seconds to
  answer, like a real conversation. At the end, a summary card shows your answers next to the
  right ones.

You don't need perfect spelling: close answers and answers that mean the same thing count too.
The app shows other natural ways to say it.

## Keep it fresh

- **🔁 Review:** phrases come back just before you'd forget them (after 1, 2, 4, 7, then 15
  days), mixed from all your lessons. A few minutes a day is enough.
- **📈 Progress:** your test scores, accuracy over time, and how many phrases you know.

ahead.

## Tips

- **Speaking works best somewhere quiet.** If the app warns about noisy surroundings, move
  somewhere quieter, hold the phone closer, or switch to ⌨️ Type.
- **Listen to yourself:** after a spoken answer, play back your own recording.
- **On a phone,** keep the screen on during Time attack.
- **Progress stays in this browser.** Clearing browser data, or switching to another device or
  browser, starts you fresh.
- The speech check confirms the right phrase came out clearly. It doesn't grade your
  pronunciation.
